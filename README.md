CineStream Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

CineStream Analytics is a Power BI dashboard that tracks viewership, engagement, monetization, and content performance for a streaming platform. It compares current-year performance against 2023 across volume, revenue, engagement quality, subscription mix, device usage, and regional distribution — giving stakeholders a single view of how the platform is growing and where that growth is coming from.

🎯 Key Insights

Volume & Engagement - 
2,000 total streams and 2,304.9 watch hours, up 15.3% and 18.7% respectively vs. 2023.
Average completion rate: 55.5% (+4.3 points YoY) — viewers are finishing more of what they start compared to last year.

Monetization - 
Total revenue and ARPU ($25.64) both grew roughly 17–18% YoY, indicating that spend per user is keeping pace with raw streaming volume growth rather than lagging behind it.

Subscription Mix - 
Premium subscribers make up under half the base (46.7%) but are the largest single driver of revenue — monetization is concentrated in the top subscription tier.

Device & Region - 
Smart TV and Mobile are the two leading viewing devices.
East Africa, Europe, and North America all contribute meaningfully to stream volume, with no single region dominating.

Content - 
Drama leads in stream volume and anchors the top of the Content Performance Ranking, led by the title "The Last Signal."
The Budget vs. Completion scatter plot shows no clear pattern between higher production budgets and higher completion rates — spend doesn't reliably predict engagement.
